One planner, every season

Every 30 minutes, and whenever new prices arrive, the pilot plans the next 24–36 hours: the cheapest slots charge, the most expensive ones are covered from the battery, and the hours in between keep the energy for later. The same inputs always give the same plan.

Winter arbitrage

Night power at 0.10, peaks at 0.45 and 0.50. Charging pays because 0.10 ÷ 0.9 efficiency + 0.10 spread = 0.21 is well under 0.50, and the day at 0.20 is not worth spending on.

Summer PV export

The roof fills the battery by noon, so the pilot leaves it to the sun. With export mode on, the evening peak is sold to the grid.

Dunkelflaute

Dark, still weeks bring the widest spreads. An eightfold price swing within one day makes grid charging pay for itself, even after conversion losses.

The plan on your dashboard

A Lovelace card ships with the integration: planned actions, price, consumption and state of charge in one view, with three layouts from a phone column to a wall panel.

Six steps behind every plan

  1. Price forecastReads the price entity of your tariff integration: 15-minute or hourly slots, today and tomorrow.
  2. Consumption forecastA small model trained daily on your own history: hour, weekday and, with a heat pump, outdoor temperature.
  3. PV forecastExpected solar production comes off the demand, and surplus refills the battery in the simulation.
  4. OptimisationPairs the cheapest charge slots with the most expensive hours, within capacity, power, SOC limits and efficiency.
  5. ExecutionAt each slot boundary it calls your scripts: charge, block discharge, auto or export.
  6. AccountingFrom your battery meters it reports what the battery is worth and, separately, what the pilot added.

Careful with your battery, honest about the money

Vendor neutral: the pilot drives your inverter through Home Assistant scripts you write, so it works with any battery Home Assistant can control.

  • Learns your consumptionTrained daily on your history, aware of weekdays, temperature and heat pumps.
  • Uses the PV forecastWill not lock the battery on a day the sun refills it anyway.
  • Plans around your carPlanned evcc sessions show up ahead of time, and the battery can stay out of the car.
  • Backup reserveA floor arbitrage never spends, refilled by a deadline after an outage.
  • Cold-weather chargingPlans with the charge power a cold battery really accepts, and learns it.
  • Two honest numbersWhat the battery is worth, and what the pilot added on top.

Install tonight, watch it plan tomorrow

It ships switched off and in dry-run, so the first days are only a plan to read.

  1. In HACS, open the menu, choose Custom repositories and add this repository as an Integration:
    https://github.com/cygnusb/ha-smart-battery-pilot
  2. Install Smart Battery Pilot and restart Home Assistant.
  3. Add the integration: price sensor, battery, four control scripts, consumption meter and PV forecast.
  4. Put the card on a dashboard, and switch the pilot on once the plan makes sense to you.
